01 / POINT OF VIEW

Clear systems. Distinct interfaces.
No borrowed identity.

NOVA/FORM is a fictional design system built as a public front-end study. The goal is simple: make structure, motion, and visual personality feel intentional across every screen size.

04 / ABOUT THE STUDY

Why systems
matter.

This project is intentionally presented as a concept rather than a fictional agency with invented business credentials. The identity exists to give the interface a coherent world to operate in.

The interesting part is the system underneath: responsive composition, motion with reduced-motion fallbacks, persistent theming, interactive project details, and a lightweight Three.js hero.
